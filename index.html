About

Hi, I'm the person who'll actually build your site.

Christensen Digital is just me — no account managers, no outsourced developers, no confusing invoices. I started this business because I kept seeing local businesses either stuck with outdated, do-it-yourself websites or quoted thousands of dollars by agencies that didn't understand what a small business actually needs.

I build websites that look great, load fast, and are easy for you to understand — no jargon, no upsells you don't need. You'll always know exactly what you're getting and what it costs, and you'll always be able to reach me directly.
